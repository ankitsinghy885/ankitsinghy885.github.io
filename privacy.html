Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker · last updated 14 November 2026

Short version: this app has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Everything you type stays in your own device’s storage. We never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login and no user profile.

What the app stores, and where

The expenses, budget, categories and settings you enter are saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device. This data:

  • never leaves the device, and is not uploaded to any server;
  • is not shared with us, with advertisers, or with any third party;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app’s storage;
  • can be exported by you at any time from Settings (JSON backup or CSV).

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS or files, other than the file you pick yourself when restoring a backup, and the file it hands back to you when you export one.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. It loads its own files once and keeps them in an offline cache. It makes no network requests to us or anyone else while you use it, and it contains no advertising, tracking pixels or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
